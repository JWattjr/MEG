# MEG demo video script

Target length: 90–120 seconds. Record the public Studio Next app after the fresh contract addresses are configured. Do not show private keys or describe a scheduled match as settled.

1. **0:00–0:12 — product premise.** Open the home page. Say: “MEG turns one esports series into nine independent calls, one signed grid, and an auditable GenLayer result.” Point to the Studio Next chip and the nine-cell board.
2. **0:12–0:28 — real fixture.** Show Team Liquid vs Paper Rex, best-of-three, the verified-upcoming label, Map 1 veto pending, and the source-backed match identity. Explain that pending fields remain pending.
3. **0:28–0:48 — play.** Use Guided Play or Quick fill, then show one option selected in each TEAM, MAP, and PLAYER cell. Mention the common, medium, and rare rows.
4. **0:48–1:03 — economics.** Show the exact ledger: 15% common pools, 30% medium pools, 45% rare pools, 5% jackpot, and 5% platform revenue. Explain that invalid cells refund their affected stake.
5. **1:03–1:22 — wallet and proof.** Review the immutable grid. Open the Transaction Kit fee review, sign the native-GEN entry, and show submitted → accepted → finalized status with the transaction ID.
6. **1:22–1:45 — decentralized judgment.** Open `/genlayer`. Show the registered evidence URLs, strict nine-cell result structure, reason code, and resolver status. Explain that validators independently read approved public evidence and the contract persists consensus before settlement.
7. **1:45–2:00 — close.** Show `/integrity` or `/rules`. Say: “The frontend never chooses a payout. The resolver supplies the consensus record, and the MEG contract owns escrow, settlement, claims, and refunds.”

If the wallet is not funded, record the read-only proof and fee-review surfaces and state that no entry is confirmed until the contract state changes. Never fabricate a successful transaction.
