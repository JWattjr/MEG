# MEG demo script

Target duration: 90–120 seconds. Use the verified upcoming Valorant fixture; do not describe pending map veto or outcomes as settled facts.

1. Open `/` and point out the MEG wordmark, Studio Next chip, wallet state, and the nine-cell grid.
2. Show the fixture strip: Team Liquid vs Paper Rex, BEST OF 3, Map 1 veto pending, and the verified-upcoming label.
3. Open Guided Play and select one option in each cell. Call out that common, medium, and rare rows carry different pool weights.
4. Show the “exact allocation before signing” ledger: nine independent pools, jackpot, platform share, and total stake.
5. Complete the review. Explain that the immutable confirmation records the packed grid, target round, network, and allocation before the wallet prompt.
6. If public addresses are configured, sign on Studio Next and show Transaction Kit’s fee review, acceptance, and finality states. If not, show the explicit “Studio Next lock unavailable” state and explain that no transaction is attempted.
7. Visit `/matches`, `/entries`, and `/rankings` to show that empty or unconfigured contract reads are not replaced with invented live data.
8. Visit `/genlayer` and explain the strict proof record: nine `cell_id` values, option indexes, `RESOLVED` or `INVALID`, evidence summaries, source references, and reason codes.
9. Finish on `/rules` or `/integrity`: invalid cells refund their affected allocation, full refunds exist for underfilled or timed-out rounds, and claims cannot be repeated.
